# Rong Zhang

**Battery intelligence · State-of-health estimation · Transfer learning · Energy management**

I am interested in data-driven methods for lithium-ion battery health estimation and intelligent energy management in electrified vehicles. My work combines battery experiments, time-series modeling, transfer learning, optimization, and engineering-oriented evaluation.

## Research interests

- Lithium-ion battery state-of-health estimation and prognostics
- Transfer learning and deep learning for degradation time series
- Electric-vehicle and plug-in hybrid energy management
- Battery thermal management
- Optimization, control, and data-driven modeling

## Selected research

### Cross-Rate Lithium-Ion Battery SOH Prediction

A GJO-TCN-Transformer transfer-learning framework for predicting the full-life state of health of high-rate pouch cells using knowledge learned from multi-source public battery datasets.

- Combined temporal convolution and Transformer-based sequence modeling
- Evaluated across four cross-battery transfer settings
- Implemented and evaluated in MATLAB
- Manuscript under revision at the *Journal of The Electrochemical Society* (JES-116943.R1)

*The associated research repository remains private while the manuscript is under revision.*

### [PHEV Energy and Thermal Management](https://github.com/zr057/PHEV-Energy-Thermal-Management)

Energy-management and battery-thermal-management analysis under the CLTC-P driving cycle, including CD-CS, ECMS, Thermal ECMS, and dynamic programming.

- Compared rule-based, optimization-based, and thermal-aware strategies
- Examined fuel economy, battery use, and thermal behavior
- Implemented in Python
